# Losing Access to a Team Workspace

If your seat on a Claude Team or Enterprise workspace is removed by
an admin, you lose access to that workspace's shared conversations,
projects, and settings immediately.

## What you can do
- Contact your workspace's admin or IT owner directly — only they can
  restore a removed seat.
- If you believe the removal was accidental, ask the admin to re-add
  your email from the workspace's **People** settings.

## What support can do
- Confirm whether a workspace exists and whether it is currently
  active on a paid plan.
- Support cannot restore individual seat access on behalf of a user,
  add someone back to a workspace without an admin's request, or
  override an admin's decision about who has access.

This is a permissions boundary by design, to protect workspace admins
from unauthorized access changes.
