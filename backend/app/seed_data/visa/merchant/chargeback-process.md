# How Chargebacks Work for Merchants

A chargeback is a transaction reversal initiated by a cardholder's
bank on the cardholder's behalf, separate from a merchant's own
refund process.

## Typical stages
1. **Dispute filed** — the cardholder contacts their bank, not the
   merchant, to dispute a charge.
2. **Provisional credit** — the cardholder's bank may credit the
   disputed amount while investigating.
3. **Merchant response window** — the merchant's bank notifies the
   merchant, who can submit evidence (receipts, delivery confirmation,
   correspondence) to contest the chargeback.
4. **Resolution** — the case is resolved in favor of the cardholder
   or the merchant based on the evidence submitted.

## For merchants
Respond within the window your payment processor specifies — missing
it typically results in an automatic loss of the dispute regardless
of the underlying facts.
