# My Card Was Lost or Stolen

If your card is lost or stolen, report it to your card-issuing bank
immediately — Visa does not issue cards directly and cannot block or
freeze a specific card.

## Immediate steps
1. Call the phone number on the back of a different card from the
   same bank, or your bank's app/website, to report the card lost or
   stolen.
2. Ask your bank to block the card and issue a replacement.
3. Review recent transactions for anything you don't recognize and
   flag them to your bank as unauthorized.

## Zero Liability
Visa's Zero Liability policy means cardholders are generally not
held responsible for unauthorized transactions made with a lost or
stolen card, provided the loss is reported promptly and the account
is in good standing. Liability determinations are made by the
issuing bank, not by Visa directly.
