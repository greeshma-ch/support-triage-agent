# Candidate Score and Rejection Review Requests

HackerRank does not make hiring decisions. Test results and
recommendations are shown to the hiring company, and any decision to
reject a candidate is made by that company, not by HackerRank.

## What HackerRank support can do
- Confirm whether a test was submitted successfully and when.
- Provide a candidate with their own raw score report, if the hiring
  company has enabled score sharing for that test.

## What HackerRank support cannot do
- Overturn or influence a hiring company's decision.
- Share another candidate's data or comparative rankings.
- Re-open a submitted test after the window has closed, without the
  hiring company's explicit request.

If a candidate believes their submission was scored incorrectly due
to a platform bug (not a hiring decision), escalate to engineering
with the submission ID and timestamp.
