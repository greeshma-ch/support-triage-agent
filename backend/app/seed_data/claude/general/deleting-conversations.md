# Deleting a Conversation

You can permanently delete any conversation from your own account.

## Steps
1. Open the conversation you want to delete.
2. Click the options menu (···) next to the conversation title.
3. Select **Delete**, then confirm.

## What happens after deletion
- The conversation and its messages are removed from your account
  and are no longer visible to you.
- Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone from within the product.
- Deleting a conversation does not automatically delete it from
  backups retained for a limited period for safety and legal
  purposes; see the privacy policy for retention details.

Deleting a conversation you started in a shared team workspace only
removes it for you if the workspace is configured for personal
conversation ownership; otherwise, contact your workspace admin.
